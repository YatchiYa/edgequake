## Residual-risk statement

**KV drop (W2 / Wave D) is implemented in-tree** (migration 125 + confirm-drop + soak). It is **not** a future design risk — it is a **residual-ops / release** risk until a tag ships 106–125 and fleets upgrade with write-stop replicas (R-27) and backups (R-23).

The remaining deliberate irreversible commitment ahead is **W3 source-relation / typed-embedding retirement** — the chunk-vector **read/write cutover is now implemented and flag-gated** (typed `chunk_embeddings` populated + verified), but the `eq_*_vectors` drop remains open (no new irreversible migration this turn; live fleet still `eq_*_vectors` by default). Everything short of that irreversible drop is flag-reversible. For fresh HEAD installs, R-01 backfill is largely historical; for **v0.22.0→HEAD** upgrades with large KV corpora, the one-time backfill window remains the largest operational residual (engine-throttled, stoppable at batch boundaries).

## Risk-review cadence

- Each wave's entry gate includes a re-read of this register; likelihoods updated with measured evidence (e.g., R-01 downgraded after the 1M-row ladder passes).
- A risk realized becomes a finding (new `F-091-XX`) with a test, or is annotated **mitigated / residual-ops** here (R-17..R-30). The register must never grow without a corresponding mitigation landed in the same release.
