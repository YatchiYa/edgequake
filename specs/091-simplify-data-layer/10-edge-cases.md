# 10 — Edge-Case Register

> Every edge case the design must survive, each with its mitigation and the test that proves it ([11](11-e2e-test-matrix.md)). ECs are acceptance criteria: a wave may not exit while any of its ECs is unmitigated.
>
> **Status (HEAD after Waves A–D):**
> - **EC-28..EC-36** — mitigated in-tree (Wave D / QW hardening); covered by `e2e_spec091_wave_d`, deletion e2e, fairness/budget contracts, soak.
> - **EC-17..EC-27** — queue/admission; largely covered by existing `e2e_spec091_queue_*` / contract binaries ([11 § Exists today](11-e2e-test-matrix.md#exists-today-run-these)).
> - **EC-01..EC-16** — W1–W5 / engine ladder; many owning tests are still **planned** names in [11](11-e2e-test-matrix.md). Mechanisms exist for several (write-stop, drop guard, 42P01), but named chaos/recall/1M suites are not all wired — do not treat unchecked planned tests as green.

## Edge-case stress ladder

| Ladder rung | Chunks | Workspaces | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L0 | 0 (fresh) | 1 | EC-15 no-op correctness |
| L1 | 1k | 1 | smoke; dual-read counters |
| L2 | 100k | 10 | throttle/ETA honesty |
| L3 | 1M | 50 | deletion-residue proof (M-4.2), backfill acceptance |
| L4 | 10M | 200 | scale acceptance ([07 — Migration Engine](07-migration-engine.md)); TOAST/vacuum behavior |

Every EC runs at L1; EC-01/02/05/08/09 additionally at L3; scale-dependent ECs (08, 11, 16) additionally at L4.
