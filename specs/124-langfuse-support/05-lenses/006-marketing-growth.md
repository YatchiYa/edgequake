# Lens 006 — Marketing & Growth

## Positioning

EdgeQuake is a production RAG engine. Langfuse support closes the “can I observe my LLM stack?” objection for teams already standardized on Langfuse (21 of Fortune 50 per [langfuse.com](https://langfuse.com/)).

## Messaging angles

1. **Parity** — Graph RAG + Langfuse, not graph RAG *or* observability.
2. **Open** — MIT Langfuse + EdgeQuake self-host story.
3. **Ops-ready** — Prometheus stays; Langfuse adds GenAI depth.

## Docs / changelog bullets (when shipping)

- Optional Langfuse OTLP/HTTP export (`LANGFUSE_*`)
- Settings → Open in Langfuse
- Nested retrieval + generation spans

## Avoid

Claiming “full Langfuse suite” (prompts/evals) in v1.
