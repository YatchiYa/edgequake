# Lens 006 — Marketing and Growth

## Narrative

EdgeQuake lets teams **choose** Vision quality or EdgeParse speed — and the product keeps that promise. Auto is an advanced opt-in for cost savings, never a silent surprise.

## Positioning

| Message | Audience |
|---------|----------|
| “Your parser setting is a contract” | Trust / enterprise |
| “Auto mode for born-digital savings” | Cost-conscious power users |
| “Workspace and tenant defaults that stick” | Multi-team SaaS |

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Marketing “smart routing” without saying it requires Auto.
- Implying EdgeParse is the default when UI shows Vision.

## Growth loop

Honest lineage → fewer “wrong extract” support tickets → higher retention on PDF-heavy workspaces.
