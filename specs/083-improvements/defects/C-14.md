## 1. WHY

Duplicate graph nodes for same real-world entity (THE COMPANY vs The Company; John's vs John; curly apostrophe).

---

| Field | Value |

---

## 3. Root cause (first principles)

Article strip case-sensitive before upper; both strip_suffix arms ASCII 0x27; no NFC/U+2019; case fold after strips in wrong order.

---

## 4. ASCII causal diagram

```

  "THE COMPANY" -/strip/-> THE_COMPANY
  "The Company" --strip--> COMPANY  => TWO NODES
  fix: casefold first --> company --> strip --> COMPANY
```

---

## 5. Solution (SOLID + DRY)

NFC → casefold → strip articles/possessives (ASCII + U+2019) → underscore → UPPER. Dedup migration merge duplicates. DRY: single normalize_entity_name SSOT.

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| S | Own the invariant in one module named in the solution |
| D | Depend on SSOT helpers (SchemaReadiness, TenantContext, RetryExecutor, TokenEstimator, …) |
| DRY | No second copy of normalize / retry / GUC / score scale |

---

## 6. Edge cases

CJK; hyphenated names; already-written dupes
